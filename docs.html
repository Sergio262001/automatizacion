Our Implementation Process

Building enterprise-grade automations requires a structured approach. We don't just connect apps; we re-engineer your operational workflows to maximize efficiency.

Phase 1: Discovery & Audit

Before writing any code or setting up webhooks, we must fully understand your business logic.

The Workflow Interview

We sit down with your key stakeholders to map out the current manual processes, pain points, and bottlenecks.

The Architecture Blueprint

We deliver a comprehensive diagram showing exactly how data will flow across your systems, detailing all triggers and actions.

Once the blueprint is approved, we move to the build phase.
